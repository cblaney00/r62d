# R62D features — what he asked for, and where it lives in the code

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-16, word for word. One bullet per feature: what he asked or chose, then the code names. Search this file (grep) for a feature, a function or a test name. Newer dated work is in docs/history.md.

His wishes so far:
- Seven maps picked in the lobby (Harbor Office, Cliffside Villa, Rail Depot, The Embassy,
  Alpine Lodge, Clocktower School, and Dust Town: he asked for a classic CS-style desert town, `shared/maps/dust.js`, mostly one floor, `FL.SAND` ground with a `sand()` texture). 51 operators (he chose 15 each first, then asked for at least 5 per role: 21 more were added, see "New operators" below), covering R6's role types with our own names and twists.
  Quick Match only (first to 3 rounds, sides swap every round). Defenders choose the site during operator select.
- Out of sight = dull grey "remembered" map; only what YOU look through is live colour (he asked, no more shared team vision): your eyes, the drone/camera feed you watch (yours or a teammate's), the teammate you spectate (`teamViewers` in main.js, plus open-hatch and stairs-preview viewers derived from it). Teammates' pings/spotted marks still show; a dead teammate gets a big red ✖ + name through walls (`deathMarks`). Enemy changes out of sight stay hidden until seen (`st.known` vs `st.map` in main.js).
- Drone sprint (he asked): Shift on a drone, `DRONE_SPRINT` in constants.js (1.6x, 3 s, 3 a round, 6 s wait), server `p.sprints`/`sprintUntil`/`sprintCd` in tickPlayer, `mods.sprint` in physics `stepMove`, mine `ds`/`dsl`/`dsc`, louder `droneLoop`. Drones are `DRONE_R` 2.5, drawn ×0.7, hit radius 4.5. `node tests/dronesprint.js`.
- Controls are CS2-style (players come from CS2): run default, Shift walk, Ctrl crouch (hold), C lie down (toggle; `o.pr`/`PRONE_T` in physics, `map.under` + `UNDER_KINDS`: drones and low eyes pass/see under desks, cars etc., `castSight(..., low)`), E use, Q skill, 3 melee, 4 gadget. Walking/crouching are silent to others. Running spreads shots (server `fire`). Mouse is pointer-locked in matches with sensitivity + ADS multiplier settings.
- Before you click in (or after tabbing out) the mouse is not captured and your aim simply stays put (it used to follow the loose pointer and bounce with the camera, he noticed); a pulsing "CLICK TO TAKE CONTROL" (`#clickIn`) shows, and that first click only takes control (`st.lockAsked`), it doesn't shoot. The camera snaps (no swoop) when its target jumps >420 px, and the sightline look-ahead settles more gently.
- Aiming mode "Rotate" (`spin`, added when he still found aiming bad; like Steel Storm / an FPS): the view turns so you always face up the screen (`st.rot` = -π/2 - padAng; `w2s`/`screenToWorld`, the draw matrix `TA..TD`, `#mapLayer` CSS `matrix()`, fog all use it), mouse X turns, Y sets reach (`turnMove`), movement keys are sent with `k.rot` and turned in physics `stepMove` so W walks up the screen, audio pan follows the view (`setListenerRot`). The camera is not clamped to the floor edge while turned (fog blacks out beyond). Text stays upright: `fillText`/`strokeText` on the game and top canvases are wrapped to undo the drawing's rotation round their spot, and labels above things use `UP()` (screen-up in the world). Without a 2× scope the pad/turn/rotate crosshair reaches 4 m further (he asked).
- Aiming style Face (he asked, he chose: only the character turns, 30°, a choice in Settings): `face` in `AIM_MODES`, `faceMove` (like `turnMove`; while aimed in `padAng` stays within `FACE_ADS` 15° of `st.faceC`, the facing when you aimed in), `moveTurn()` turns the keys (Rotate, Face, sprint + W) for `k.rot` and `vaultDir`; thin arc drawn in `crosshair()`.
- Aiming defaults to **Crosshair** (`free` mode, he found the pad horrible): the crosshair moves 1:1 with the mouse like a pointer but is fixed to your character (`aimOff`, `freeMove` in main.js), so walking or the camera sliding never changes your aim; it passes straight over your character (2026-11-15, he asked): within `DZ_R` (the old 46 CSS px stop) your aim holds its direction (`st.dzAng`, `aimAng()`/`dzHeld()`/`turnAim`/`limitAim` in main.js, `S.dzA` in armory.js) unless a seen enemy's body is within `DZ_FOE` 0.5 m of the crosshair (he chose: near the crosshair, nothing extra drawn), and coming out the other side turns you; it stops at the screen edge with no hidden travel. Old profiles on the pad switch once (`aimV: 2`). The older modes stay in Settings → Aiming. Before that the default was the **aim pad** (he asked for it then): a ring on the right of the screen (`pad()` in main.js) acts like a joystick — the mouse is kept inside it and its direction from the centre sets the aim, its distance how far ahead the crosshair sits (`cursor()`), so turning round is a short flick. No dead middle (he asked): captured-mouse moves go through `padMove` (sideways always turns, straight through the middle comes out the other side). Settings → Aiming also has "Turn" (FPS-style: mouse X turns, Y sets reach, `turnMove`) and "Cursor" (aim at the cursor); saved per profile (`aim`).
- Xbox controller (he asked): `public/js/gamepad.js` (read each frame by `pollPad` in main.js; layout listed in Settings). Right stick aims like the pad, left stick moves (light push walks), B toggles crouch.
- Crosshair v2 (he asked: the old thin white one was hard to see): green cross + dot with a black border, pixel-sharp (`crosshair.js`); old saved crosshairs reset to it once (`XH_VERSION`).
- Builds follow armor (`BODY_W`/`HIT_R` in shared/operators.js): heavies broad, mediums and lights skinnier. Bullets hit within `hitR(op)` (6.6 / 6.0 / 5.5 px since 2026-11-06, he asked skinnier; `BODY_W` 0.8/0.71/0.64), smaller than the bumping radius `PLAYER_R` (now 6), so people are harder to hit. He asked for this.
- Movement uses real-world speeds (`M_S` in shared/physics.js, 1 m = 36 px = `PX_PER_M`): jog 2.6 m/s (2.3 heavy, 3.0 light), walk 1.4, ADS 1.5, crouch 0.9, downed crawl 0.5, drone 3.2. He asked for everything slower and realistic.
- See as far as you can be shot from (he asked): the camera measures the 80th-percentile sightline across your view cone (`st.farS`, grows fast, shrinks slowly), zooms out just enough (up to 680 world px tall) and slides ahead (`st.sightLead`, smoothed `st.leadV`) so that far point is on screen; small rooms stay close. (The red ! edge mark for off-screen enemies was removed: he asked for fewer alert marks.)
- FOV like a modern FPS (103°, 70° ADS). Aiming is quick (`ADS_TIME` 0.1–0.28 s) and now WIDENS the view (×1.12, 2× scope ×1.3, `st.adsZ`; he asked later: zooming in was the opposite of what you want); he asked for quick aiming and for less slow panning, so the room-size zoom (`st.envWant`/`st.envH`) only changes for a >15% difference and settles in ~0.5 s. Zoom adapts: close indoors, wider outside / long sightlines (`want` in the main.js camera: 240–470 world px tall indoors, up to 520 down a sightline; he asked twice for closer).
