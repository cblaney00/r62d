# 1V1 and the small maps (2026-11-20, he asked)

## What he asked and chose
- "add a 1v1 mode, study jynxzi's series on 1v1's … limit it to small maps only" and "add some 2 floor maps that
  would be good for 1v1ing … popular … not a staple of the default game".
- Research: Siege's own 1v1 playlist is Bomb on House, Clubhouse and Oregon, 30 s prep, a ban every round, at most 7
  rounds; the official 1V1 NOW tournament (Xbox Game Pass, 2026) played Chalet and Clubhouse. Jynxzi's 1v1s
  (him against streamers and viewers) are custom games on those small maps. Community favourites for 1v1: House,
  Clubhouse, Oregon, Chalet, Kafe; small footprint, two floors, tight hallways, a few vertical angles.
- He chose: Siege's 1v1 rules (first to 4, sides swap every round, 30 s prep, each player bans one operator before
  every round, full kit); new maps = a suburban house and a lake cabin, in 1v1 AND the normal 5v5 rotation; the 1V1
  card on the FUN MODES page.

## How it is built
- `shared/modes.js` `duel`: `kind: 'siege', fun, size: 1, small: true, winRounds: 4, swap: 'round', bans: 'round'`.
- server/game.js: `teamSize()` (rules.size, never in tutorial/free play/TV) replaces TEAM_SIZE for bots/joins/switch;
  `smallOnly()` + `fitMode()` (choosing 1V1 sends extra bots away and moves to a small map; big maps refused; random
  map only from `MAPS[k].small`); alone at the start → a bot opponent. `playerBanStep` with `bans: 'round'`: every
  round the attacker bans a defender and the defender an attacker, `this.bans` cleared each round (bans last one
  round); ban lines name the player. Reinforcements are two a defender (`REINF_EACH * teamSize / TEAM_SIZE`: 10 in
  5v5, 2 in 1V1).
- bots.js `botPrep`: alone (`solo`), reinforcing is done first (task `pri`, picked before nearer tasks in `prepWork`)
  and only the site's own doors are boarded (boarding every door ate the whole 30 s: 0.5 walls reinforced a round
  before, 1 after).
- Client: FUN_LOOK `duel` (sage ⚔), lobby map list only small maps, empty slot "a bot plays you, unless a friend
  joins", `soloMode(s)` words in the ban picker (YOU / YOUR OPPONENT, "this round") and spawn choice (no REQUEST
  DEFUSER, RANDOM = a random spawn).
- Maps: `shared/maps/house.js` (Maple Street House: garage, living room, kitchen with a back stair, dining, den,
  bathroom; kids room, master bedroom with a balcony, study, guest room, laundry; 4 sites) and `shared/maps/cabin.js`
  (Lakeside Cabin: great room open to the loft above (`U.drop`), kitchen, pantry, mudroom, den, bunk room, a
  boathouse by the lake joined by a breezeway, outside timber steps to a west deck; 4 sites). `small: true` in
  `MAPS`; outdoor scenes in shared/outdoors.js.
- mapkit `ratSpots`: a hiding cabinet is never put in a drone hole's line (it blocked holes on both new maps, and
  one on Harbor Office, which lost that hiding spot).

## Tests
- `node tests/duel.js [n]`: rules, lobby (one a side, small maps only), a bot opponent when alone, bans to the side you
  face, whole bot matches (ends at 4, ≤7 rounds, sides swap every round, 2 bans every round, banned never picked).

## The playtest (two passes, 2026-11-20)
Pass 1, screen: the ban picker and spawn choice talked about teams ("your team", "this match", REQUEST DEFUSER,
"the team spreads out"); the lobby showed an empty opponent slot. Fixed (above). Pass 2: defending round 1 on the
cabin, ban step, site choice, prep and action all read right; no page errors.

Pass 1, play (8 Diamond-bot matches): a lone defender spent prep boarding every door and reinforced 0.5 walls a
round. Fixed (above). Pass 2 (12 matches, 73 rounds, Diamond bots): **attackers win 45%** (target 45–55), action
lasts ~30 s, first kill ~38 s, plants in 16% of rounds (~44 s in), the defender is off the site 46% of the time,
attackers drone in prep every round and in action ~1.2 times a round, the defender watches cameras. Pro bots:
52 s rounds, 40% attack (25 rounds); Copper: 21 s rounds.

Things that (almost) never happen in a bot 1v1, and drills that could teach them (ideas, not built):
- **Ropes and windows from above**: 1 rope in 73 rounds, no roof or window entries. Drill: one attacker on the roof,
  one defender holding a 2F room with a window; points for a rappel entry or a window peek that gets the kill.
- **Hatches and floors**: no hatch opened from above, no shooting down through a floor. Drill: the defender sits
  under a soft floor or hatch; the attacker scores for opening it and taking the fight from above.
- **Playing the plant**: attackers plant in 16% of rounds and nearly all rounds end in the first gunfight. In real
  1v1s the attacker often plants to force the defender to come to them. Drill: attacker with the defuser and 40 s,
  defender roaming; points for a plant and holding it rather than hunting.
- **Soft wall play**: attackers rarely open walls in a 1v1 (barricade breaks are the bulk of "break" sounds). Drill:
  a reinforced site with one soft wall; points for opening a new angle instead of walking the door.
- **Defender skills a lone defender never uses**: SOLACE (no teammate to heal), and AUDIT, GIRDER, WARD, ARGUS,
  TONIC, RAMPART, SOLDER showed 0 uses in 1–6 rounds each. Some are team skills by nature; the others are worth a
  look in `botPrep` / `botThink` for a defender playing alone.

## Personalities and small talk (2026-11-20, he asked)
- He asked: do bots keep their personalities in 1v1 (yes: roster `a`/`p`, liked jobs, favourite operators, habits all
  apply; checked with Vortex vs Hollow, Diamond, 6 matches: defending off the site 60% vs 37%, first fight 26 s vs 31 s,
  favourite operator 22/36 vs 17/36). Team habits (trading, sticking to a teammate) have nothing to act on alone.
- He then asked: the bot opponent talks to you, by personality, some more sociable than others, never annoying, not
  constant. bots.js `social(p)` (roster `c` if set, else 0.15 + 0.6·a − 0.2·p + a little of its own), `duelTalk`
  (called by game.js at prep start, a kill, round end), `duelFlush` (each tick). Up to 1–3 lines a round by how chatty (he asked: 3 at most, keep the
  20 s rule), always 20 s apart, 1–12 a match, a gg at the end (30–90%), in all chat. Lines: comms.js `DUEL` (fun = Copper/Bronze, cocky =
  pushy, calm). Phones show these (all chat), not bot team chatter. Test: `node tests/duelchat.js [n]` (Pickles ~6 a
  match, Wobbles/Hollow 0–2, none in 5v5).

## Meta test (2026-11-20, he asked: who is so strong they should be perma-banned)
`node tests/duelmeta.js 4200 5 15` (≈3 min). See docs/history.md for how it works. First result, 12,600 rounds: too strong
MASON 63%, SHUNT 62%, ECHO 62%; strong TALON, HUSH, HAZE, HOIST; weakest PERCH 36%, LOCKJAW 40%; WRAITH average (50%) with
bots, which only scan now and then (a person may get much more from it).
