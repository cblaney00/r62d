      LEARN R62D

      One quick playable run (about 4 minutes) in a private match: you attack one round, then defend one. The two enemy bots never shoot; the clock waits while the coach explains each step.

      HOW TO PLAY

      Rounds and roles, droning and pinging, moving and shooting, breaking in, rappelling and planting the defuser; then reinforcing, barricading, cameras and holding the objective.

      THE ROUND

      Operator select — pick who you play; defenders choose the objective. Preparation (30 s) — attackers drone, defenders fortify. Action (2 min 40 s) — attackers break in and plant, defenders stop them. First to 3 rounds; sides swap every round.

      ROLES

      Breachers open walls, intel finds enemies, support clears the way, entry goes in first, flankers come from behind. On defence anchors hold the objective, roamers slow the attack, trappers and intel make every door dangerous.

      INFORMATION IS KEY

      Most rounds are won before the first shot. Drone before you walk in, check cameras before you peek, listen for footsteps and breaking walls, and tell your team everything you find.

      PING ANYTHING

      Press your ping key (T unless you changed it) or middle-click (a click in a drone or camera) on an enemy, camera, trap, door, window, wall, drone hole, the objective or any spot. Your team sees a named marker and a line in team chat.
